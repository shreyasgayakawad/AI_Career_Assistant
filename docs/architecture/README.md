# AI Career Assistant

> **An AI-powered career platform that helps professionals discover jobs, optimize applications, build professional connections, and manage their entire job search from one place.**

---

## Overview

AI Career Assistant is an open-source project focused on making the job search process more efficient and organized.

Instead of relying on multiple websites, spreadsheets, documents, and repetitive manual work, the platform combines job discovery, AI-assisted document generation, referral management, application tracking, and career analytics into a single workflow.

The long-term goal is to build a **Career Operating System** that helps professionals throughout their careers.

---

## Vision

Help professionals spend less time managing their job search and more time preparing for the right opportunities.
