\## Company Intelligence Dashboard



\*\*Problem\*\*



Applicants spend too much time researching companies before applying.



\*\*Idea\*\*



Create a dedicated dashboard for every company showing:



\* Open jobs

\* Employees

\* Recruiters

\* Official careers page

\* Company overview

\* AI summary

\* Previous applications

\* Referral opportunities



\*\*User Benefit\*\*



Everything about a company is available in one place.



\*\*AI Benefit\*\*



AI summarizes the company, identifies hiring trends, and recommends the best application strategy.



\*\*Dependencies\*\*



\* Company

\* Job

\* JobPosting

\* Recruiter

\* Employee



\*\*Priority\*\*



🔴 High



\*\*Complexity\*\*



High



\*\*Status\*\*



Idea

\## Smart Referral Assistant



\*\*Problem\*\*



Finding the right employee for a referral is difficult and time-consuming.



\*\*Idea\*\*



Automatically identify employees who are most likely to respond to referral requests.



\*\*User Benefit\*\*



Higher referral success rate.



\*\*AI Benefit\*\*



AI ranks employees based on role relevance, activity, and shared skills, then drafts a personalized outreach message.



\*\*Dependencies\*\*



\* Company

\* Employee

\* Referral

\* Application



\*\*Priority\*\*



🔴 High



\*\*Complexity\*\*



High



\*\*Status\*\*



Idea
